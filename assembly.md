# Saturday, Reopened. — physical edition

Print `print-kit.pdf` on **two US Letter sheets (8½ × 11 in), portrait, single-sided, at Actual Size / 100%**. Turn off **Fit**, **Shrink**, and any multiple-pages-per-sheet setting. The PDF already contains the paper color and graphics. Ordinary white paper works; use color if available. No special stock or purchased props are needed.

1. **Check the scale.** Each card must measure **7 in wide × 5 in high** between the corner trim marks. Measure before cutting; if it differs, correct the print scale.
2. **Cut four cards.** Use the corner marks and the outside edge of each fine border. A ruler and scissors are enough. Leave each card flat; the backs are intentionally blank. The two cards on each sheet have a ¼-in gap.
3. **Stack in order, face up:** cover / 00 → Lithium / 01 → Answers / 02 → Judgment / 03. Sheet 1 contains the cover and Lithium; sheet 2 contains Answers and Judgment.
4. **Present the stack.** Use an envelope you already have with an interior larger than 7 × 5 in, wrap it in a spare sheet of ordinary paper, or hand over the stack as it is. Put the cover first. An optional handwritten envelope label is **James Cadwallader — Saturday, Reopened. — From Josh’s assistant**. No mailing or return address is supplied.
5. **Leave the response optional.** James can circle LITHIUM or WILDER on the last card and hand it back to Josh, or reply to Josh with that word. The cards do not submit or send anything.

`print-preview.png` shows the four finished cards; it is a preview, not a print master. `print-kit.html` is editable and self-contained, with inline SVG artwork and system fonts. If printing the HTML, choose US Letter, portrait, scale 100%, margins none, background graphics on, and browser headers/footers off. Use the supplied PDF when preserving the checked typography and geometry matters; its fonts are embedded. Recheck size and text after edits.

The card artwork measures exactly 504 × 360 PDF points (7 × 5 in); both PDF pages measure 612 × 792 points. Text, source URLs, trim marks, and all four card renders were checked digitally. A physical printer was not operated, so final size still depends on the print settings above.
